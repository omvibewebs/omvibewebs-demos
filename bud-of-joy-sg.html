Our story

From home oven to Circuit Road

Ten years, one starter, zero preservatives.

2016

A starter named Bubbles

Founder Joy Lim begins baking sourdough in her MacPherson HDB kitchen. Neighbours queue down the corridor.

2019

Certified organic

We switch every ingredient to certified-organic sources and earn our organic certification — flour, butter, chocolate, the lot.

2023

71 Circuit Road opens

Our first real bakery-cafe: stone deck oven, 20 seats, and the smell of fresh sourdough drifting down the street.

Today

30+ wholesale partners

Cafes, schools and grocers across Singapore serve Bud of Joy daily — and Bubbles the starter is still going strong.
