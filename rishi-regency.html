Welcome to Rishi Regency

Where Jabalpur's warmth meets quiet luxury

Minutes from Madan Mahal Fort and the ghats of the Narmada, our hotel blends old-world courtesy with modern comfort. Wake to filter coffee on your private balcony, dine under a canopy of lights, and let our concierge plan your day — from Bhedaghat's marble rocks to Dhuandhar's mist.

Every corner is crafted for unhurried evenings: handloom upholstery, brass lamps, and service that remembers your name.
