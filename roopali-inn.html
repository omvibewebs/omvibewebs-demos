Our Story

A Napier Town institution, run like family

Since 1989, Roopali Inn has been where Jabalpur comes for honest vegetarian food — generous thalis, smoky tandoor and recipes that regulars have loved for generations.

01

Pure vegetarian kitchen

100% vegetarian, always. Aromatic rice, creamy curries, sizzlers and family feasts — made fresh through the day, never reheated shortcuts.

02

Stay upstairs

Clean, air-conditioned rooms with Wi-Fi, parking and 24-hour reception — ideal for business and family travellers.

03

Open 10 AM – 11 PM

Lunch, evening snacks and dinner, all seven days — plus home delivery and takeaway across the city.

04

Family first

Spacious family seating, wheelchair-accessible entry and free parking — bring everyone.

05

Easy to reach

859, Station Road, opposite Income Tax Office — 2 km from Jabalpur station, near Russell Chowk.
