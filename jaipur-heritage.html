Our Heritage

Two centuries of Rajputana grace

Built in 1824 as the winter residence of a Jaipur noble family, our haveli has watched elephants process to Amer Fort and monsoons silver the Man Sagar Lake. Today its frescoed walls, marble courtyards and star-lit terraces welcome travellers seeking the Jaipur of poetry, not postcards.

Every arch was restored by master artisans from Sanganer; every suite carries a story — of durbar evenings, of monsoon raagas, of a city that has always known how to celebrate life royally.
