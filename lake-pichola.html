The Legacy

A Haveli That Has
Watched Centuries Drift By

On the peaceful island of Bramhapuri, on the western banks of Lake Pichola, stands a heritage haveli from the age of the Mewar maharanas — far from the bustle of the old town, yet a short stroll from its monuments.

From lake-facing balconies the view is unmatched: Gangaur Ghat and Bagore Ki Haveli across the water, the illuminated Chandpole bridge, the sprawling City Palace — and at night, the Jagdish Temple glowing above the ripples while folk melodies drift across the lake.

This is not a hotel beside the lake. It is a haveli of the lake — carved arches, cool courtyards, and mornings that begin with temple bells on water.

— The Lake Pichola Heritage
