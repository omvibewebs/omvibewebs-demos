Our Story

Two kitchens, one mountain heart.

Hand-pleated every morning — never frozen, never rushed.

Khana Khajana began as a six-table North Indian eatery. Mini Nepal began as a momo cart run by the Sherpa family from Pokhara. In 2018 the two families became one kitchen — and Singapore got the best of both worlds.

Today our tandoor fires at noon for butter chicken and seekh kebab, while the steamer baskets never stop for jhol momos, open chicken momos and buff sukuti. Two culinary traditions, one promise: food that tastes like somebody's grandmother is watching.
