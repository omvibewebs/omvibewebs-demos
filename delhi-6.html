Our Roots

Taste of Chandni Chowk

Delhi 6 is the pin code of Old Delhi — the walled city's beating, sizzling heart. We rebuilt its three most legendary food lanes right here in Little India.

01

Paranthe Wali Gali

Since 1872, this single lane has fried parathas in desi ghee for everyone from poets to prime ministers. Our tawa never cools — just like theirs.

Try · Paratha Platter S$18
02

Khari Baoli

Asia's largest spice market — a kilometre of chillies, cardamom and chaos. Every masala we use is ground fresh weekly, the Khari Baoli way.

Try · Masala Chai, on the house
03

Dariba Kalan

The old silver lane where jewellers share walls with 100-year-old jalebi shops. Sweet, crisp, unapologetic — that's our dessert philosophy.

Try · Jalebi Rabri S$9
