OUR STORY

FROM A CORNER
CAFETERIA TO A LEGEND

Drag through the decades — every era added a flavour Dubai still queues for today.

1979

THE FIRST SPIT

A tiny Lebanese cafeteria opens its doors. One charcoal grill, one family recipe, and shawarma like Dubai had never tasted.

١٩٧٩
1988

GARLIC SAUCE FAME

Word spreads: the toum (garlic sauce) is unreal. Taxi drivers start detouring just for a wrap. The queue becomes a landmark.

١٩٨٨
2001

THE JUICE BAR

Fresh-squeezed juices join the menu. Orange, avocado, and the famous layered cocktail — pressed to order, never bottled.

٢٠٠١
2015

FOUR CORNERS OF DUBAI

From one grill to four buzzing branches — Diyafah, Satwa, Deira, Barsha — each one packed till late, every single night.

٢٠١٥
2026

STILL THE PEOPLE'S PLACE

Same charcoal, same recipes, same welcome. Legends don't chase trends — they outlast them.
