Privacy Policy — Kidds Uploader

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Kidds Uploader is a personal automation tool built by Om Pathak. It uploads kids' educational videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (kidds fun learning) using the YouTube Data API.

What data is accessed

With your explicit OAuth consent, the app may access:

  • Your YouTube channel information (to confirm the upload destination).
  • Video files you choose to upload, along with the titles, descriptions and tags you provide.

How data is used

Data is used solely to upload and manage videos on your own YouTube channel. Nothing is shared, sold, or disclosed to any third party.

Data storage

OAuth tokens are stored securely and used only to perform the uploads you authorized. You can revoke access at any time from your Google account permissions page; revoking immediately stops all access.

Children's privacy

Uploaded videos are marked "Made for Kids" as required by YouTube's policies. The app itself collects no personal data from viewers.
