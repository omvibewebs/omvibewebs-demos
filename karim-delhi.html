The Legacy

From the Emperor's kitchen to your plate

In the mid-19th century, Mohammed Aziz cooked for the Mughal Emperor in the Red Fort. When the empire fell, his recipes travelled with the family — until his son Haji Karimuddin brought them back to Delhi and opened a humble dhaba with one dream: royal food for the common man.

1857 · The Royal Kitchen

Recipes of the Red Fort

Mohammed Aziz, khansama to the Mughal court, guards the imperial recipes through the fall of the empire and passes them to his son.

1911 · Delhi Durbar

A kiosk before Jama Masjid

Haji Karimuddin sets up a food stall for the coronation crowds, serving alu gosht and dal with rumali roti. The queues never end.

1913 · Gali Kababian

Karim Hotel is born

With his earnings, Karimuddin opens his own eatery near Jama Masjid — the flagship that food lovers still pilgrimage to today.

Today · Fourth Generation

A living legend

Run by the fourth generation of the family, Karim's now spans 50+ outlets — yet every kebab still follows the century-old hand-written recipes.
