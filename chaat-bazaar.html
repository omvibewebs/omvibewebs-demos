Our Story

FROM A MUMBAI
THELE TO KARAMA'S
FAVOURITE NOISE

Chaat Bazaar started in 2019 as a six-foot cart outside a Karama grocery store. One handi of ragda, a steel dabba of imli chutney, and a family recipe for masala that three generations refused to write down.

Today the cart is a full-blown bazaar — but the rules haven't changed: everything fresh every morning, chutneys ground in-house, and no chaat leaves the counter until it makes you grin.
