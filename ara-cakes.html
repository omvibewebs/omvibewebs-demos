Meet the Artist

Hi, I'm Jaya

Six years ago I baked my daughter's birthday cake in our little Hauz Khas kitchen — and never stopped. Today Ara Cakes is Delhi's boutique studio for couture celebration cakes: hand-painted tiers, sculpted florals and flavours people write back about.

Every cake leaves my studio only when I'd proudly serve it at my own table.
