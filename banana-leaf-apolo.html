Our Story

Half a century,
one leaf at a time.

Born in 1974 on Race Course Road, The Banana Leaf Apolo grew from a humble eatery into Singapore's beloved South Indian icon. Every meal is still served on a fresh banana leaf — the way it has been for fifty years. Fish head curry, crispy dosas, and fragrant biryanis: recipes guarded like family heirlooms.
