Our Story

Four decades of crumbs & love

What began as a two-table bakehouse inside The Claridges is today Delhi's most nostalgic address for real bread.

1982

A wood-fired dream

Master baker Mr. D'Souza fires the first batch of sourdough in a corner of The Claridges' kitchen. The queue forms before the shutters open.

1995

The croissant years

A Paris-trained laminator joins the family. Delhi tastes a true 27-layer butter croissant for the first time — and never looks back.

2008

Cakes go legendary

Our old-fashioned truffle cake becomes the city's worst-kept secret for birthdays, anniversaries and 2 AM cravings.

2026

Still slow, still ours

Third-generation bakers, the same 1982 starter culture, the same wood-fired oven. Some things should never change.
