Varanasi Experiences

A day on the ghats, curated

Our concierge desk strings these together into one seamless day — boats, bells, lanes and laddoos, all handled.

5:30 AM

Sunrise Boat Ride

Drift past 84 ghats as the city wakes — sadhus at prayer, pigeons lifting off the water, chai in a kulhad.

9:00 AM

Old City & Silk Lanes Walk

A guided wander through galis older than memory — silk weavers at wooden looms, paan, and hidden temples.

2:00 PM

Sarnath Day Trip

Where the Buddha gave his first sermon — stupas, museums and deer park, 40 minutes away with our driver.

6:45 PM

Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh

Reserved riverside seating for the fire ceremony — conches, chants and a thousand flames on the water.

8:30 PM

Banarasi Food Trail

Tamatar chaat, kachori-sabzi, malaiyo in winter and blue-lassi — our food captain knows every legendary stall.
