Our Heritage

Thirty-eight years of mehmaan-nawazi

Born as a family guesthouse in 1987, Hotel Krishna grew — arch by arch — into Napier Town's most loved address for weddings, business stays and slow family holidays.

A courtyard calm in the middle of the city

Step off busy Napier Town road and the noise falls away. Cool stone corridors, the smell of fresh marigold, a fountain murmuring in the central courtyard — Krishna was designed for unhurried days.

Our kitchen serves pure-vegetarian Central Indian and North Indian classics on banana-leaf Sundays, and the rooftop comes alive at sunset with chaat, chai and the city lights below.

— The Krishna Family
