Every one of our 32 rooms is dressed in handloom textiles, lime-washed walls and soft morning light. No two floors feel the same — each carries a quiet nod to Bengal's art and craft.

From a 6 AM airport shuttle to midnight mishti cravings, our team is built around one idea: anticipate, don't interrupt. Business travellers, wedding parties and slow-weekend seekers all find their rhythm here.
