Our Story

A legacy plated since 1983

What began as a twelve-table dining room above Raffles City is today Singapore's beloved address for North Indian fine dining — still family-run, still cooking over real fire.

1983

The first tandoor is lit

Our founder arrives from Old Delhi with a suitcase of hand-ground masalas and opens Shahi Maharani's doors at Raffles City.

1997

Recipes of the royal kitchens

The family travels through Lucknow and Jaipur, documenting heirloom recipes from khansamas of the princely states.

2012

A new generation, same fire

The second generation takes over the pass — keeping the 18-hour dal and the clay tandoor exactly as they were.

Today

Singapore's royal table

Four decades on, heads of state and homesick students sit side by side — united by butter chicken.

"We don't chase trends. We tend a fire that has been burning since 1983 — and let the flavours speak in their own royal time."

AR
Aditya RathoreExecutive Chef · 2nd Generation
