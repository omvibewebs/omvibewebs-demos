Our Story

From a Madurai street cart to Little India.

It started with one wood-fired stove.

In 1998, Kumar arrived in Singapore with his grandmother's recipe book and a stubborn belief: Madurai food doesn't compromise. The first shop was six tables on Buffalo Road. The queue, on day one, went around the block.

Twenty-eight years later, the recipes haven't changed. The chicken chettinad still gets its black pepper roasted fresh every morning. The kari dosa still lands on the tawa with a sizzle you can hear from the street. And every lunch is still served the proper way — on a fresh banana leaf.
